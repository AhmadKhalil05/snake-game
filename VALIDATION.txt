Compatibility note
------------------
Ordinary LOVE 11.5 uses LuaJIT, so highscore.lua selects love.filesystem there.
highscore54.lua contains the requested native Lua 5.4 <close> implementation;
it is selected only by a Lua 5.4-capable interpreter or a Lua 5.4-built LOVE.
